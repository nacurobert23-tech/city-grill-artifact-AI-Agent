### Recommendations

Ranked by expected impact on rating.

- **Complaint recovery script:** taste-check or replace a returned dish without debate; manager comps on the spot. Removes the most common 1★ story.
- **Track an organic rating:** report Google excluding name-only reviews, and Tripadvisor, next to the headline Google score.
- **Itemised, explained bills** and a neutral tip prompt with a visible "no tip / cash" option.
- **Offer the lunch menu proactively** in all languages at Caru' cu Bere.
- **Temperature and freshness checks** at the pass for lamb and platters at Hanu' lui Manuc.
- **Reply to every 1–3★ review** with specifics, as Caru' cu Bere already does.

**Method.** **Google Maps:** – reviews pulled via the SignalQub API on 13 Oct 2026 (Caru' cu Bere: 1,000, 30 Sep–13 Oct, "most relevant"; Hanu' lui Manuc: 4,445, 11 Aug–13 Oct, "newest"; the API returns at most \~4,445 per request, so earlier Google reviews are not covered). Reviewer names and photos removed. Themes coded by multilingual keyword matching; praise/complaint follows the star rating. **Tripadvisor:** – English-language reviews with visit dates Jan 2025 – Oct 2026, read page by page on Tripadvisor on 13–14 Oct 2026 (Caru' cu Bere: about 20 pages; Hanu' lui Manuc: about 10 pages; 15 reviews per page, older visits discarded), each paraphrased and coded per theme with +/− polarity. Tripadvisor lists reviews by its "detailed reviews" order, so this is a reading sample, not every review: it averages well below the platform's 4.1, which suggests longer, critical reviews are over-represented. Use it for themes and direction, not as the true average. Platform totals as listed on Tripadvisor and Google in Oct 2026. The samples show direction and themes, not statistically representative percentages, and the two restaurants' Google samples cover different periods.
